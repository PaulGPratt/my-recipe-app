ABOUT ME

Blake Marretta

Founder and Trainer at O4K9

​

Blake is a United States Marine Corps Veteran, having served eight years. Beginning in 2015, he learned from both Marine Corp and civilian Dog Handlers, and has worked numerous dogs of various breeds, age and size.

​

As a Marine and small business owner, Blake has an unyielding devotion to personal integrity and a commitment to task accomplishment. 

 

"I don't stop teaching you how to work your dog until you can do it like I do" ~ Blake
