    Kuranda Dog Cots

    Kuranda's elevated dog beds are a great relief for dogs’ joints. The cot-style design evenly distributes their weight so that there are no pressure points like they would have on the ground.

    The design secures the fabric inside a special channel in the frame making it totally inaccessible to dogs that chew. Unlike pillow beds that hold onto dirt, odors and pet hair, just wipe down or rinse off your Kuranda Dog Bed with a hose to clean. No more lingering odors.
