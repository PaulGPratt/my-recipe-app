O4K9 Website Proposal

The original fonts, colors and photographs are retained. Training links directly to the existing consultation page, with clear program pricing and the additional consultation fee.

What changes

  1. Clear service choices: Training, Boarding and Daycare in the same order in the menu, homepage actions and service line. The boarding action says “Book Boarding.” Actions keep their original text weight.
  2. Training begins with a consultation: the existing consultation page includes program pricing before the gallery and cancellation policy. Its required $150 fee is additional to the program price.
  3. Training Programs & Pricing: five informational program cards, a separate Reactive Rehab entry, and a Boarding & Daycare group with clear rates replace “Choose your Plan.”
  4. Make the location clear: a larger Moscow, Idaho service line under the original introduction, with a dark photo overlay for readability.
  5. Fix the boarding link: the boarding card opens boarding. Training links open the existing consultation page and its pricing. The package popup proposal was removed.
  6. Make contact visible: published phone, email and address in a simple footer using the existing styling.
  7. Clarify booking: use Dogs and Kennels in boarding, show readable booking help, and clarify daycare arrival wording.
  8. Clean up Hotel Amenities listing: remove bedroom counts, mountain view and “pets allowed” from kennel details.
  9. Search/accessibility: descriptive local titles and image descriptions.

Preview limits

All forms, accounts, dates, prices calculated for a stay, carts and confirmations are demonstrations. No live Wix scripts, tracking, booking, payments, login or messages are connected. Availability is illustrative. Final daycare hours, arrival rules, capacity, and booking terms need Blake’s confirmation. The original baseline is also inert for safe comparison.
